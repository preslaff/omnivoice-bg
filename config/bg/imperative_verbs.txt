#   Each section states its stress rule. Validate the RULE, then scan the
#   section for the flagged exceptions. Flags:

#                  `review` stage force-surfaces every one for your ear.
#     # STRESS?    I am unsure of the stress placement — please confirm/fix.

#   Every verb carries exactly one acute on a vowel (mechanically audited);
#   the # STRESS? flags are where that placement needs your linguistic call.
#   Inline text after '#' and blank lines are ignored by the parser.
